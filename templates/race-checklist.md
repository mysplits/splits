## Triathlon: what goes where

### On you at the swim start
- Trisuit (worn the whole race)
- Cap (usually from the organisers) + goggles, and a spare pair
- Wetsuit if the rules and water temperature allow it (the organisers decide on the day: bring it anyway)
- Watch in triathlon mode
- Old flip-flops to walk to the water
- Anti-chafe on neck and armpits if wearing a wetsuit

### Bike (T1)
- **Helmet, buckled before touching the bike** + sunglasses
- Bike shoes
- Bike checked: tyre pressure, gears, brakes, bottles in place
- Race number on a belt (back on the bike, front on the run)
- Bottles: drink mix + water. In the heat, more electrolytes
- Fuel taped or boxed on the frame
- Puncture kit: tube, levers, CO2 or pump (mandatory in non-drafting races)
- Small towel for the feet

### Run (T2)
- Running shoes (elastic laces save time)
- Cap or visor
- Socks if you change
- Fuel for the run and for after the finish

## Transition flow (single transition)
| Moment | What you do |
|---|---|
| Set-up | Bike in your spot; helmet and glasses on the bike; bike and run shoes lined up; fuel in place |
| T1 swim → bike | Out of the water → helmet buckled → bike shoes → bike → cross the mount line → ride |
| T2 bike → run | Dismount before the line → bike in your spot → run shoes + cap → out |

## Variant: separate transitions (T1 and T2 in different places)
1. Pick up the bags and set up **T2 first** (run kit).
2. Take the bike, bike kit and swim kit to **T1**.
3. Respect the kit check closing time (often about 25 min before the start).
4. Out of the water: cap, goggles, wetsuit and flip-flops go into the T1 bag; the organisers move it to T2.

## Rules to remember
- Non-drafting: keep 10-12 m to the rider in front, or penalty box.
- Helmet buckled from before touching the bike until it is racked in T2.
- Find a visual landmark for your spot in transition.
